"This murder mystery competition has revealed that although NLP ([natural-language processing](./Natural-language_processing)) models are capable of incredible feats, their abilities are very much limited by the amount of [context](./Context_(linguistics)) they receive. This [...] could cause [difficulties] for researchers who hope to use them to do things such as analyze [ancient languages](./Ancient_language). In some cases, there are few historical records on long-gone [civilizations](./Civilization) to serve as [training data](./Training_data) for such a purpose." (p. 82.)
